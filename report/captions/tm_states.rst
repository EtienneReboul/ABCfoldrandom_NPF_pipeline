Outward-open (OF), occluded (Occ) and inward-open (IF) states read from the **extremities** of the TM-Ca PCA cloud (section 3), not from its density: in PC1-PC2 the ensemble draws a V (tip up or down) or a lambda (one arm joins the other before its end, the occluded end sticking out), and a GMM or HDBSCAN splits the dense arms and misses the sparse tip. Fitted models in sparse, isolated spots are trimmed (fewer than {{ snakemake.config["tm_states"]["mass_frac"] * 100 }} % of the models within {{ snakemake.config["tm_states"]["radius_frac"] * 100 }} % of the cloud diameter; dark grey). The corners are the largest triangle on the hull of the remaining models. **Occluded** is the corner lying on (V) or just off (lambda) the path the data draw between the other two (geodesic on a kNN graph). **OF vs IF** compares the two other corners on extracellular minus intracellular opening, measured as the distance between the N-bundle (TM1-6) and C-bundle (TM7-12) Ca centroids of the helix halves on each leaflet: a relative test between the two ends, with no gate residue pair; the TM1-7 / 2-11 / 4-10 / 5-8 gate distances must agree. Every model gets weights alpha to the three corners (left panel colour = their mix); its state is the largest. Right panel: the bundle openings of the models by state, the independent check (OF open outside only, IF inside only, Occ at the closed corner). Title in red = **unresolved**, with the failed checks (no clear tip, near-linear cloud, OF/IF ends too similar, the two opening definitions disagreeing, or the occluded corner not the tightest). Circled: the {{ snakemake.config["tm_states"]["n_export"] }} models per state written to ``results/<protein>/tm_states/structures/``, spread over the alpha range of the state's members with alpha >= {{ snakemake.config["tm_states"].get("alpha_floor", 0.8) }} (models partway along an arm, between two corners, are left out; 01 = most extreme), superposed on the mean TM frame: one ``.pdb.gz`` each in ``pool/``, symlinked from ``OF/``, ``Occ/``, ``IF/``.
